About me

I'm a PhD candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in Civil and Environmental Engineering at the George Washington University, where I conduct research at the GW Transportation Lab. I received my Master's degree in Transportation Engineering from École nationale des ponts et chaussées and my Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering from the University of Tehran.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Intelligent Transportation Systems. I focus on developing AI-driven approaches for understanding, modeling, and predicting complex transportation systems, with particular interests in Large Language Models, spatiotemporal learning, physics-informed modeling, autonomous vehicles, and transportation safety.

Project

AI-Powered Traffic Safety and Analytics Platform

In collaboration with Leidos and the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA)

The platform combines large-scale traffic detector data, machine learning, and a large language model fine-tuned for transportation analysis. It uses historical traffic patterns and their variation over time to identify roadway operating conditions and assess safety risks.

Project objective

Bring transportation engineering, AI, and data science together to help practitioners use traffic data to anticipate roadway safety risks and make informed decisions about traffic operations.
